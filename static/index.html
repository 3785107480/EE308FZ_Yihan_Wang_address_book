A place for every detail.

Bookmarks

Select the star beside a contact to bookmark them. Open Bookmarks in the sidebar to find your important contacts quickly.

Multiple contact methods

Each person can have multiple labeled phone numbers, emails, social handles, postal addresses, websites and other details. Open a contact to see every method.

Excel import & export

Export includes all contacts, bookmarks, notes and contact methods in one row per person. Repeated columns keep every value and label. Phone numbers are saved as text to retain leading zeros.

Import requires an .xlsx workbook with a Contacts sheet. Use the blank template or fictional sample. Invalid rows reject the entire file; exact duplicates are skipped. A contact may have up to 100 methods. A file may contain up to 5,000 contacts and be no larger than 5 MiB.
